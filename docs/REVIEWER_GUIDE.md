# Try the demo

Start at [onground-arnav.vercel.app](https://onground-arnav.vercel.app). You can explore the app without entering a phone number. The store leads and metrics are sample data.

Here's a quick route through it:

1. On **Dashboard**, open **Review allocated leads**. Check Mukunda's buying signals, then move to the next lead. Open **Performance & earnings** for the KPIs and charts.
2. In **Calls**, expand a customer and change pages. **Incoming** has a callback action on each missed call. **Recent** includes the labelled recording of the real recovery test.
3. Open that call review and save its suggested callback request. Go to **Calendar**, select the requested date, and check that the details carried across.
4. In **Pulse**, open a channel, answer the pinned request and send a message. Try the role agents for practice, task planning or a workspace question.

The callback is a request for the store to confirm. Messages and tasks stay inside your demo workspace.

## If you want to try calling

Use your own Indian mobile number, including `+91`, and confirm the call when prompted. For the recovery scenario, play a shopper who liked a mattress but wants to discuss price before coming back. The AI introduces itself as a demo assistant.

The trial provider may need to verify your number first, and the demo has a shared call allowance. If calling is unavailable, the recorded test is in **Recent** and in the [1:50 walkthrough](https://onground-arnav.vercel.app/submission.mp4). Browser voice practice uses your microphone instead of a telephone number.

The original recovery call ran for 88 seconds. Human calling, voice practice and the follow-up were also tested; the [README](../README.md#what-works) has the results. [Why I built it this way](EXPLAIN.md) covers the product decisions.
